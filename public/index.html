Verify a Certificate

The certificate ID is printed on your certificate. Letters and numbers only; hyphens allowed.

How It Works

  1. Find the ID. Locate the certificate ID printed on the student's certificate.
  2. Enter it above. We check it against the academy's official records.
  3. Review the result. Confirm the name, course, and issue date match the certificate in front of you.

If the details do not match, or an ID is not found, please contact the academy using the details below.
